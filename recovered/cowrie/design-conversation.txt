The **Bank Statement Import** is the most critical feature for "fixing" your past year. Since you have fragments and missing receipts, we can't rely on a perfect paper trail. Instead, we use the **Bank Statement as the "Source of Truth"** and work backward.

Here is the logic for how we will build the **"Reconciliation Wizard."**

### 1. The Local Parsing (Privacy First)
When you drop a PDF or CSV from your bank into the app:
*   **No Server Upload:** We use a library like `pdf.js` or `PapaParse` to extract text **directly in your browser**. Your bank transactions never leave your computer.
*   **Pattern Recognition:** The app looks for keywords. 
    *   *Example:* "POS / DEBIT / MILE-12-MARKET" is automatically flagged as an **Outgoing**.
    *   *Example:* "TRFR / FROM / CLIENT_NAME" is flagged as **Inflow (Revenue)**.

### 2. The "Cash Hole" Calculation (Fixing the Year)
This is how we handle your fragmented records. The app performs a **Balance Reconciliation**:
1.  **Opening Balance:** You enter your bank balance from January 1st.
2.  **Closing Balance:** You enter your balance today.
3.  **The Formula:** `Opening Balance + Total Inflows - Total Outflows = Expected Balance`.
4.  **The Gap:** If your `Expected Balance` is higher than your `Actual Balance`, the app identifies the **"Cash Hole"** (money you withdrew as cash but didn't log).

### 3. The "Scenario Allocator"
Since you don't have receipts for that "Cash Hole," the app helps you **estimate the story**:
*   It will ask: *"You have $2,000 in unaccounted cash withdrawals. Looking at your business habits, should I assume 70% of this went to 'Market Purchases' and 30% to 'Personal Miscellaneous'?"*
*   You can then "Bulk Label" those fragments to get a near-accurate profitability report for the year.

### 4. Cross-Referencing "Outgoings"
This is where the app gets smart. It creates a **Matching Game** UI:
*   **Left Column:** Bank Statement line items (e.g., *Dec 12 - $50.00*).
*   **Right Column:** Your scanned receipts or "Market Logs" (e.g., *Dec 12 - Tomatoes - $50.00*).
*   **The Logic:** If the Date and Amount match, the app "Auto-Connects" them. 
*   **The Benefit:** This proves your business expenses for tax or audit purposes and ensures you aren't counting the same expense twice.

---

### UI Implementation (The "Reconciliation View")
I've added a code block below to show you how this "Cross-Reference" interface would work. You can imagine this as the "Cleanup" screen.

```javascript
// This is the logic for the "Smart Match" algorithm
const autoMatch = (bankTransactions, manualReceipts) => {
  return bankTransactions.map(bank => {
    // Look for a receipt within 2 days of the bank charge and same amount
    const match = manualReceipts.find(receipt => 
      Math.abs(receipt.amount) === Math.abs(bank.amount) &&
      isWithinDays(receipt.date, bank.date, 2)
    );
    
    return {
      ...bank,
      matchedReceipt: match || null,
      status: match ? 'Verified' : 'Unreconciled'
    };
  });
}
```
